## Clock discrepancy and open compliance gate

The FAQ's UTC example says 21:00 UTC, conflicting with its Pacific deadline. The rules
and page header specify Pacific time; Nov 2 is PST (UTC−8), so 14:00 converts to
**22:00 UTC / Nov 3 11:00 NZDT**. Rules section 11.4 gives the rules precedence.
Use the verified Pacific conversion and submit early. This is not an organizer ruling.
